What happens inside?

Every machine changes both your image and your walking body. The slider previews a change. Apply commits the processed image as the next input and makes the bodily change persist. New effects accumulate; Reset clears them. The body uses geometric and material interpretations of each image algorithm, rather than applying image-matrix operations directly to a 3D mesh. Walking leaves data fragments after a change is committed.

SVD
SVD: low-rank reconstruction of a real grayscale matrix.
FFT
2D FFT: remove high frequencies, then reconstruct from the remaining spectrum.
Sort
Pixel sorting: reorder contiguous pixel segments above a brightness threshold.
Bits
Extract one binary bit from each RGB channel.
Mosh
Block displacement and temporal feedback simulate compression failure; video encoding is not modified.
Flow
Video uses Horn–Schunck brightness-based optical flow. Static images use gradient flow fields.
Stone
Image edges, noise and cracks form sandstone. Choose Strata in Materials for flowing geological textures.

Keep pins your processed head to the Visitors Board. Copies stay on this browser across visits; Next visitor clears the current input, not the board. Remove individual heads or use Clear board to delete them. Only copies you Keep are stored; unsaved inputs and camera streams are not saved or uploaded. Session fragments still clear on reset or close. The avatar and room are procedural low-poly models driven by p5.js. Sound starts muted. Reduced-motion preferences are respected.
